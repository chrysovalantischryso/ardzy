How to use these projects

Each project is a complete FPGA design for the S9 control board, ready to upload: a prebuilt bitstream, a Python demo, a self-test that measures every part on your own board, and a guide. In the Ardzy app open Ready projects, pick a card and press Upload and run, Self-test or Guide. Copy to my projects gives you an editable copy: change the Verilog or the program, press Build FPGA (the built-in toolchain, no Vivado needed) and Upload.

All projects share one layout on the ARM side: a 64 KB register window at 0x43C0_0000 cut into 16 slots of 4 KB. Slot 0 is always the Info block (project name, seconds, the 4 LEDs). blocks.py has a Python driver for every block; the Verilog blocks are in the app's FPGA library (fpga/hdl) and can be combined in your own designs.

Every self-test passed on the real board. Most need nothing connected: the FPGA reads its own pins back at the pads, and test generators inside stand in for sensors. The FM radio (project 12) sends radio signals: read its guide about the rules before you put an antenna on it.
